# Page Topology

1. **Fixed backdrop**: 32px paper grid, warm radial glow, two slow decorative orbs.
2. **Reading progress**: desktop-only fixed rail at the left edge with two-digit percentage.
3. **Header**: compact Oppenheimor identity at left and GitHub destination at right.
4. **Hero**: 86vh desktop / 74svh mobile, three-line display statement, metadata and scroll hint. Original seed-orbit illustration overlaps the final line.
5. **Garden tabs**: five categories in a horizontal tablist. Active tab has a thick yellow underline. Mobile tablist scrolls horizontally.
6. **Panel area**: one tabpanel at a time. Content enters with opacity and translate motion. Projects uses a bordered two-column feature card; other panels use ruled lists or compact editorial blocks.
7. **Elsewhere**: section number, outlined background wordmark, label and ruled destination rows.
8. **Footer**: copyright and “still growing” status.

All sections remain in normal document flow except the backdrop and desktop progress rail. The tab panel is click and keyboard driven; the progress rail is scroll driven; entrance reveals are intersection driven.
